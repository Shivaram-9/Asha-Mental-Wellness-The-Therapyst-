About Asha

20+ years of compassionate care and transformation

BA Child Psychology
M.A. Clinical Psychology & Psychotherapies
PGD in Family and Marital Therapy

Asha Suhasini is a globally affiliated Senior Mental Wellness Consultant and Psychotherapist with over 20+ years of dedicated clinical and corporate experience in transforming lives and fostering emotional well-being.

Asha's work is grounded in the potential of the subconscious mind and the healing power of evidence-based psychotherapy. She holds a Bachelor's in Psychology (specialising in Child Psychology), a Master's in Clinical Psychology from S.N.D.T. Women's University, Mumbai, and a Post-Graduate Diploma in Family and Marital Therapy. She is a registered counselling professional with the Counsellors Council of India (CRN9047214) and a registered member of the International Association of Holistic Psychology.

Her career began at Bandra Family Court, Mumbai (2003) as an Assistant Psychotherapist preparing unbiased psychological reports to assist judges and couples in divorce, reconciliation, and child custody cases. This was an additional psychological counselling services to benefit the divorce and reconciliation cases.

Over her career she has combined strong academic foundations with extensive practical experience across clinical, educational and corporate settings.

Languages Known: English, Hindi, Marathi, and Telugu (Fluent professional proficiency).
